## Report a job

Found a closed posting, a wrong location or salary, or a job that is not a software role? [Open a job report](https://github.com/codemind-hq/software-jobs-us/issues/new?template=report-a-job.md) with its link, or use **Report** on its CodeMind page. These pages are rebuilt from CodeMind twice a day, so an edit to them would be overwritten: a report is how a job gets fixed.

## About this list

Software engineering jobs from employers' own career sites, posted in the last 7 days and refreshed twice a day. Each one is checked: a real employer, a software role, and open to people in the US. A remote job is listed only when the posting says it hires in the US (or North America or the Americas); on-site and hybrid jobs are in the US. Internships and new-grad roles are not included.

*Apply Directly* opens the employer's own posting. *Details & Match* opens the job on [CodeMind Jobs](https://jobs.codemind.site/?utm_source=github-software-us), which shows how well your resume matches it.

## About CodeMind

[CodeMind Jobs](https://jobs.codemind.site/?utm_source=github-software-us) is a job search site for software engineers. On it you can:

- [Browse the complete job database](https://jobs.codemind.site/jobs-in/united-states?utm_source=github-software-us), not just this week's jobs.
- [Discover jobs that match your resume](https://jobs.codemind.site/register?utm_source=github-software-us) with a free account.
- [Check your ATS score for free](https://jobs.codemind.site/ats-checker?utm_source=github-software-us): how an applicant tracking system reads your resume.
- Tailor your resume to a specific job, from the job's Details & Match page.
