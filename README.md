## Report a job

Found a closed posting, a wrong location or pay, or a job that is not a software role? [Open a job report](https://github.com/codemind-hq/software-jobs-us/issues/new?template=report-a-job.md) with its link, or use **Report** on its CodeMind page. These pages are rebuilt from CodeMind twice a day, so an edit to them would be overwritten: a report is how a job gets fixed.

---

**About this list.** Software jobs from employers' own career sites, posted in the last 7 days. Each one is checked: a real employer, a software role, and open to people in the US. A remote job is listed only when the posting says it hires in the US (or North America or the Americas); on-site and hybrid jobs are in the US. Internships and new-grad roles are not included.

**Links.** *Apply* opens the employer's own posting. *Details & Match* opens the job on [CodeMind Jobs](https://jobs.codemind.site/?utm_source=github-software-us), which shows how well your resume matches it.
