## About this list

A remote job is listed only when the posting says it hires in the US (or North America or the Americas); on-site and hybrid jobs are in the US. Internships and new-grad roles are not included.

*Apply Directly* opens the employer's own posting. *Details & Match* opens the job on [CodeMind Jobs](https://jobs.codemind.site/?utm_source=github-software-us), which shows how well your resume matches it.
