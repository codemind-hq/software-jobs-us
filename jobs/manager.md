## About this list

Software engineering jobs from employers' own career sites, posted in the last 7 days and refreshed twice a day. Each one is checked: a real employer, a software role, and open to people in the US. A remote job is listed only when the posting says it hires in the US (or North America or the Americas); on-site and hybrid jobs are in the US. Internships and new-grad roles are not included.

*Apply Directly* opens the employer's own posting. *Details & Match* opens the job on [CodeMind Jobs](https://jobs.codemind.site/?utm_source=github-software-us), which shows how well your resume matches it.
